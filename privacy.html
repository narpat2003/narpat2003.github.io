Privacy Policy: Lore Knower Uploader

This is a private tool. Only the channel owner uses it, to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel.

It collects no data from anyone else.

It stores only the owner's own sign-in token, kept privately, and uses it only to upload and schedule the owner's videos.

It shares nothing with anyone and sells nothing.

Its use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. Questions: github.com/narpat2003
